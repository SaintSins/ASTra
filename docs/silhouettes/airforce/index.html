Nike Remasters the Air Force 1 to its Early-2000s Glory

Nike is officially digging into its archives to bring back the "Golden Era" of the Air Force 1: the coveted 2001 shape. After years of pleading from sneakerheads and collectors, the Swoosh is reviving the exact mold that solidified the all-white "Uptowns" as a cultural and streetwear phenomenon.

For nearly twenty years, the widely-produced '07 shape has been the standard for the Air Force 1. The remastered Air Force 1 '01 (Style Code: IV4501-100) reconstructs the early-2000s silhouette with noticeable upgrades in material quality and overall slope.

Here is how the revived '01 shape compares to the standard '07 version:

  • Leather Quality: The '01 features premium, buttery-soft tumbled leather that creases naturally, replacing the stiffer, heavily coated leather of the standard '07 model.
  • Toe Box: The silhouette returns to a slimmed-down, aerodynamic shape that tapers downwards, moving away from the chunkier, taller toe box of recent releases.
  • Collar: The '01 utilizes thicker ankle padding lined with nylon, compared to the thinner, mesh-lined padding on the '07.
  • Heel Profile: The revived shape offers a smooth, curved heel design rather than the more squared-off and rigid back of the standard version.
  • Packaging: The '01 pairs ship in the classic two-tone orange and brown Nike box instead of the modern standard grey box.

Instead of reinventing the wheel, Nike is tapping into the energy that catapulted the AF1 to stardom in the early aughts. The return of the premium materials and the lower-profile toe box feels aimed directly at purists who have spent years hunting down deadstock pairs from 2001.

The Air Force 1 '01 "Triple White" is expected to retail for $130—a slight premium over the standard general release due to the upgraded leather. While exact release dates vary by region, pairs are expected to launch throughout the summer and fall of 2026 via Nike SNKRS and select retailers. Rumors also point to additional colorways and collaborative models utilizing this '01 build through the remainder of the year.
